## Decisions locked in

- **Multi-page routing.** Pecorella's three at `/part-i`, `/part-ii`, `/part-iii`. Modern lives under `/modern/<topic>` — multiple focused pages, not one mega-page, because Pecorella's per-part discipline is the load-bearing trick.
- **No raw plots** — every chart figure is a Vue demo island parameterized by the data behind it. If we'd draw a static line, we render it interactively instead.
- **Engine on demand** — static curves use `Decimal` math directly. Engine instances reserved for time-evolving sims (production-over-time, prestige cycles).
- **Paraphrase + cite** — not verbatim reproduction. Source links per page.
- **Source spreadsheets** — `docs/source/Idle Game Models (GDC Talk)/*.xlsx` are the formula source-of-truth for Part I–III. Demos transcribe directly from them.
- **Modern register switch.** Modern pages are _distillations_ from the research corpus, not paraphrases of a single source. Tone is "Pecorella stopped here; here's what the genre learned." Demo density may drop in favor of context/text — undecided per-topic; one anchor demo + narrative is fine when text is the point.
- **Length discipline.** Per-topic target ~2500–4000 words (Pecorella's range). Overrun = signal the topic is actually two topics; split rather than dilute.
- **Citations.** Modern pages cite upstream GitHub/GitLab paths with line numbers and pinned SHAs. `src/lib/cite.ts` resolves a `{ repo, path, line }` triple at build time against `../open-loop-research/.gitmodules` + `git submodule status`. `<Cite>` for inline; `<Sources cites={[...]}>` for the footer block. Both shapes coexist with the legacy `post`/`sheets` shape.
- **One global sidebar.** `Sidebar.astro` renders site nav (grouped by section) + per-page TOC, both collapsible. Reused on every page including `/`. Mobile is a hamburger drawer with scrim. Index page is content + sidebar; no separate `/modern` hub page — the sidebar is the hub.
- **`pages.ts` shape.** Sections concept (`label`, `blurb`, `pages[]`); `partChain` is the flatmap; `adjacent()` walks the flat chain unchanged. `PagePointer.description` provides the one-line hook shown on the index landing cards.

## Phase 4 — Modern evolution (post-Pecorella, since Oct 2016)

Pin date: Pecorella Part I published 2016-10-04. Modern = ~10 years of evolution since.

Slug + read-order chain (locked in `pages.ts`, stubs in place under `src/pages/modern/`):

1. `/modern/big-numbers` — break_infinity (2018) → break_eternity (2020). The substrate. Foundations for everything below.
2. `/modern/softcaps` — sublinear gear-shifts at thresholds; the universal modifier on Pecorella's pure exponentials. Research: 62% piecewise prevalence (50-game spray test). Four canonical shapes, each anchored to a distinct lead example so the draft doesn't fall back to AD-everywhere: **sqrt softcap** → Realm Grinder faction bonuses; **log softcap** → NGU Idle (the genre's most softcap-dense game, multiple shapes coexist); **piecewise log** → Leaf Blower Revolution (the leaf softcap is a genre meme); **hard-then-soft** → Trimps helium / fluffy XP. Specialty case worth surfacing: Exponential Idle's late-theory `f(x)` softcaps where the softcap is the math motivation, not a balance patch. AD replicanti softcap as supporting example only — not the lead.
3. `/modern/cost-acceleration` — AD's quadratic-in-exponent: `base × mult^owned × scale^(excess·(excess+1)/2)`. The canonical post-Pecorella cost curve.
4. `/modern/layered-prestige` — nested resets where each layer rewrites the prior loop's math, not just multiplies it. Lead on **Prestige Tree** (Jacorb's project that generalized layered prestige into a build-your-own framework) and the **TMT lineage** it spawned (Modding Tree, Dimensional Incremental Rewind, Incremental Mass Rewritten, Distance Incremental). That's where layered-prestige became a _pattern_ rather than a single game's mechanic — the historical anchor is the engine framework, not any one game using it. Depth case: **Synergism** — five+ live reset layers (ascensions → singularities → octeracts → ambrosia) plus corruptions as a sideways modifier mutating run shape per ascension; the deepest live example by a clear margin. Survey: NGU rebirths → metas → evil → sadistic (five-layer escalation); AD's Transcendence → Eternity → Reality as one ladder among several (no longer the reference); Cell to Singularity narrative-shaped tiers; Universal Paperclips phase transitions as story-shaped layered prestige.
5. `/modern/modifier-chains` — Profectus/TMT lineage. Additive → multiplicative → exponent ladders as a first-class abstraction. Engine's `Formula` class is exactly this. Engine-history spine kept (Cookie Clicker hand-rolled `Game.CalculateGains` → Prestige Tree per-layer `gainMult` → TMT named slots → Profectus `createSequentialModifier` chain-as-data). Game-side depth examples added so the page is grounded in actual gameplay, not only engine archaeology: **NGU** energy stacks (power × cap × bar × speed × per-zone × adventure × titan...), **Trimps** (heirloom × perk × scientist × challenge × heliolith), **Realm Grinder** (faction × pact × spell × research × abdication), **Synergism** (corruption × challenge × buyable × research × upgrade × singularity). Concrete witnesses to "the chain is real, here's what 8-deep looks like in production." Citable code: Synergism `allWowCubeStats` (a literal 14-entry array of named modifier objects, structurally identical to Profectus's chain-as-data), Trimps `applyMultipliers(multipliers, stat, ...)` taking a key→fn map of 16+ entries for trimp damage. NGU lacks clean source; cover it as a high-level catalog. RG is flash-extracted; cite via `EXTRACTED_FORMULAS.md`.
6. `/modern/time-as-resource` — Pecorella's `t` is an axis; modern `t` is a currency. Four pins:
   - **Pin 1 — Zero-sum time budgets (anchor demo, the project namesake).** Groundhog Life's 1440 min/loop, Omsi Loops' 250 mana where mana = time (`baseManaPerSecond = 50`, `curAction.manaUsed++`), Cavernous II's action queues. Math is knapsack: `Σ duration_i · count_i ≤ T_budget`, maximize `Σ reward_i · count_i`. Demo: drag-bars allocator across 3–4 activities with marginal-yield curves. Source: `patterns/tier-1-foundation/time-systems.md:485-575` (Variation C).
   - **Pin 2 — Speed multipliers as effective time.** AD's tickspeed (the genuine time mechanic in AD, not Eternities), Progress Knight 2's 10,000×. Math: `effective_time = ∫ speed(t) dt`, with the feedback wrinkle that speed itself is bought from production → exponential cost has to tame the runaway. Demo: production-over-time with speed slider + toggle for "speed buys feed back from production" to show the divergence. Source: `patterns/tier-1-foundation/time-systems.md:416-482` (Variation B).
   - **Pin 3 — Action duration as mutable state.** Cavernous II / Omsi Loops sub-pattern: `actionDuration = baseDuration × specialMult × realmMult`, training reduces base duration. Order of operations matters because earlier actions reshape later costs. Math: a planning problem where the time-cost function evolves with use. Demo: 4-action queue, skill levels visible, drag-to-reorder, total-time readout.
   - **Pin 4 — Time as decay/pressure (text-led, one anchor demo).** Increlution's `health = 0.5 × 1.25^(seconds/60)` makes time the opponent — exponential decay forces termination at ~60 min. Optimization shifts to "maximize progress before death." Demo: progress-vs-time under varying decay exponents with the optimal-action-mix changing along the curve.
7. `/modern/offline-progress` — the math of being away. Four families: **delta-time extrapolation** (`rate × elapsed`; Cookie Clicker with 5s timeout cap, AD), **tick simulation** (run the loop; Melvor 24h full sim, NGU, Synergism 200-tick budget, Shark Incremental accelerated 12–20x), **bonus-time banking** (offline time becomes a spendable currency burned during active play; KTL `bonusTime`, Trimps, Cavernous II at 50% efficiency), and **the cap** (universal — 3h to 72h). #6 made in-game `t` a resource; #7 asks what wall-clock absence converts to. Banking is the spicy reframe — it keeps the active loop primary instead of turning the player into a returning spectator.
8. `/modern/production-graphs` — Pecorella's Part II cascade is a linear chain (tier `i` produces tier `i+1`); modern games run multi-currency conversion DAGs and sometimes cycles. Adjacency-matrix view of "X produces Y at rate r"; steady-state via dominant eigenvalue when linear (Part II's `t^d/d!` is the chain-length-`d` special case); cycles introduce feedback — runaway or fixed point depending on softcap placement. Capstone for Part II the way #3 is for Part I. Lead on **Universal Paperclips** (paperclips → autoclippers → trust → memory → operations → creativity) — a clean six-node graph readers can hold in their head, and a non-AD anchor with explicit narrative-driven topology shifts. Depth and density examples: **Synergism** (particles/quarks/runes/talismans/ascension/singularity — densest currency graph in the corpus); **NGU** (energy ↔ magic ↔ R3 with boosts feeding all three — explicit cycles); **Cell to Singularity** (each tier produces previous + special currencies); **Realm Grinder**, **Distance Incremental**, **Incremental Mass Rewritten** as TMT-derivative graph examples. AD as one of several, not the centerpiece. **Excludes loop-game action structures** — Idle Loops, Omsi, KTL have _scheduling_ DAGs (actions consuming time/mana), not currency-production graphs; those belong in #6's mutable-duration thread, not here. Earlier pin conflated the two; corrected.
9. `/modern/synergy-multipliers` — modifier chains (#5) are scalar operators applied sequentially to one number. Synergies are _set functions_: multipliers that depend on the **combination** of owned things, not the count. Cookie Clicker grandma types unlocking each other, Realm Grinder faction synergies, AD dimension-boost interactions, Synergism corruption combinations. Math is set-function maximization — often submodular, which is why these games _feel_ solvable (greedy is near-optimal). Genuinely orthogonal to #5. Stub `achievement-as-modifier.mdx` was repurposed → `synergy-multipliers.mdx` (the original framing was a thin restatement of #5; this delivers what it gestured at, generalized).
10. `/modern/automation` — Pecorella's `1c–1e` Optimal Choice answers "which generator do I buy next?" statically. Modern games turn that into a UI surface and a _policy_. Three canonical policies — **buy-when-affordable**, **buy-max-each-tick**, **threshold-based** — produce different production-curve shapes. Priority-N autobuyers create regimes: low priority → all tiers grow together, high priority → collapses to top-tier-only. Convergence question: does dynamic autobuy approach the static-optimal answer, or systematically deviate? Sibling to #3 the way #8 is sibling to Part II. Lead on **Trimps** (the most-elaborated automation system in the genre — heirlooms, perks, autostance, autoportal, autostructures; automation IS the late-game progression vector) and **Bitburner** (automation-as-genre, literal programmable scripts; a category that didn't exist pre-Pecorella). Survey: Melvor (auto-eat / auto-anything as core); Realm Grinder (auto-cast spells with priority sliders); Clicker Heroes (auto-clickers as resource); Synergism (auto-everything tiered through ascensions); NGU (BAE / advanced training / advisor); AD's autobuyer priority sliders is one row of the table, not the table.
11. `/modern/challenges` — challenge runs as a first-class modern mechanic; Pecorella has zero on it. Structure the page around **four genuinely different shapes**, not "AD's pattern with variants":
    - **Single-completable** — AD challenges: do once, get unlock; sequential progression through a fixed list.
    - **Stackable-debuff** — Synergism corruptions: multiplicative, persistent across ascensions, an additive ladder of difficulty multipliers that _compose_ rather than swap.
    - **Parallel re-run** — NGU challenges: multiple slots, full re-runs from scratch under constraints; constraint-as-content.
    - **Challenge-as-content-zone** — Trimps Spire / Mayhem / Pandemonium: challenges that ARE the content, not modes layered over the main game.
      Plus **Realm Grinder** R-challenges as the combinatorial case (challenge × faction × research stacking). The design math: how do you build a hard mode that produces a _different curve shape_, not just a slower one? Disabling a layer changes the production-graph topology (cf. #8); raising an exponent shifts the regime (cf. #3); corruption stacking is its own modifier-chain problem (cf. #5). Prestige cycles run the same loop bigger; challenges run _different loops_. AD as one of four shapes — not the reference shape with three variants.
