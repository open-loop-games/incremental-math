# Production Graphs

Pecorella's Part II cascade is a linear chain: tier `i` produces tier `i+1`. Modern games
run DAGs and sometimes cycles — Antimatter Dimensions' antimatter ↔ dimension boosts ↔
infinity points ↔ eternity, Synergism's particles/quarks/runes/ascensions, NGU's
energy/magic/R3. The general shape is an adjacency-matrix view of "X produces Y at rate
r"; Part II's `t^d/d!` is the chain-length-`d` special case.
