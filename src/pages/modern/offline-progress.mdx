# Offline Progress

The math of being away. Four families: **delta-time extrapolation** (`rate × elapsed`;
Cookie Clicker, AD), **tick simulation** (run the loop; Melvor, NGU, Synergism, Shark
Incremental), **bonus-time banking** (offline time becomes a spendable currency burned
during active play; KTL, Trimps, Cavernous II), and **the cap** (universal — 3h to 72h).
Banking is the spicy reframe — it keeps the active loop primary instead of turning the
player into a returning spectator.
