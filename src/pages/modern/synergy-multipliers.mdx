# Synergy Multipliers

Modifier chains apply scalar operators sequentially to one number. Synergies are *set
functions*: multipliers that depend on the **combination** of owned things, not the count.
Cookie Clicker grandma types unlocking each other, Realm Grinder faction synergies,
Antimatter Dimensions dimension-boost interactions, Synergism corruption combinations.
